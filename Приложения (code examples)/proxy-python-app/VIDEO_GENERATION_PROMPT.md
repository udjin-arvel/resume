Основной промт (template)

5 second cinematic commercial video based on the provided photo of [PRODUCT]. 
The product stays almost in the same position and proportions as in the original photo. 
Shot on a professional studio set with soft diffused lighting, high resolution, sharp focus, realistic colors, no stylization. 
The camera makes a very slow [CAMERA MOVE] around the product, like in a high-end product commercial, with smooth motion and no sudden jumps. 
Background: [BACKGROUND/EXTRAS DESCRIPTION]. 
No change to the product shape, label, logo or text, everything stays clean and readable. 
Depth of field is subtle, with the product in perfect focus, giving a premium cinematic look.


Где ты подставляешь:
[PRODUCT] – “jar of orange-flavored lip balm”, “women’s beige trench coat on a model” и т.п.

[CAMERA MOVE] – “push-in (slow zoom in)”, “push-out (slow zoom out)”, “slight orbit from left to right”

[BACKGROUND/EXTRAS DESCRIPTION] – либо “simple clean studio beige background”, либо детально, как с апельсинами.


Негативный промт (чтобы не было багов и мультяшности)
Кидай это почти всегда в поле negative / remove:

blurry, low resolution, distorted face, warped product, stretched logo, extra limbs, deformed hands, flickering, jitter, glitch, frame skipping, fast camera shake, motion blur, cartoon, 3d render, anime, illustration, painting, plastic skin, AI art style, oversaturated colors, strong filters, text, captions, subtitles, watermark, logo overlay

Как сделать все видео плюс-минус однотипными
Чтобы ролики были в едином стиле, просто фиксируй параметры:
Длина: всегда “5 second cinematic commercial video”.


Свет: “professional studio set with soft diffused lighting, clean shadows, realistic colors”.

Камера: выбери один тип движения, например:

для товара: “very slow push-in” или “very slow push-out”;

для людей: “very slow push-in from medium shot to closer portrait”.

Фон: для большинства товаров – “simple clean studio background in [light color]”.

Реализм: всегда добавляй фразы типа:

“no stylization, no CGI look, no cartoon, realistic 4K commercial footage”.

Subtle reflections on the cap and the tube, clean studio look, shallow depth of field with the product in perfect focus, realistic textures, premium cinematic style, no text on screen.
