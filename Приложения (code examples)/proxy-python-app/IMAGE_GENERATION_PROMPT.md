Универсальный промт “модель + вещь”
Используй, когда у тебя есть:
Image A – модель
Image B – вещь (свитер, кардиган и т.п.)

Realistic high-resolution fashion photo composition.

Use the first image as the base: a [DESCRIPTION OF MODEL: age, gender, pose, clothing] on a simple studio background. 
Use the second image as the product reference: a [DESCRIPTION OF GARMENT: color, type, texture].

Dress the model from the first image in the garment from the second image. 
The garment must look natural on the body: correct perspective, realistic fit and drape, no distortion, no cut-off areas, no mixing with the original top. 
Preserve the original design of the garment: same color, knit pattern, texture, neckline, cuffs and length as in the reference image.

Keep the model’s face, body, pose, hairstyle and expression exactly the same as in the base image, without changing identity or proportions. 
Keep any other clothing items that are not replaced (for example jeans) unchanged.

Lighting should look like clean professional studio lighting: soft, even, with natural skin tones and gentle contrast. 
Use a simple neutral studio or plain background that fits the overall color palette and keeps the focus on the garment. 
Photo-realistic commercial fashion image, no AI-art look, no special effects, no added text or logos.
